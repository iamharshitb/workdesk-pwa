How WorkDesk fits together
The bottom bar

Home is your week. Add creates tasks. Calendar plans communications. Projects follows each project. Report summarises the work.

Your profile

Tap your initials at the top right to switch Appearance between Light, Dark and Auto, turn sound on or off, and reach Ideas, Help and Backup and restore. On Home it also holds the focus timer and quick notes, and shows your streak: consecutive weekdays with at least one finished task. Weekends never break it.

Shapes and colour

The shape of a task’s mark is its status. In Light the colour is its project. Red in Light, coral in Dark, only ever means late.

This week

Mine shows what you planned for this week: Late, Today, then Later this week. Backlog, On hold and Done are folded underneath.

Mark your part done

Tap a task’s shape. An Undo appears for a few seconds. Tap the shape on a finished task to reopen your part.

Open a task

Tap the task itself to start it, put it on hold, add it to your week, set a reminder, edit its details, read the notes, and see each person’s part.

Plan your week

Add to week moves a task from Backlog into This week. Ten active tasks fit at once, and finishing one frees a place.

Team

Team lists everyone with their progress. Open a person to see their tasks. The admin can mark a teammate’s part done and plan their week.

Search

The magnifier beside the title opens search and filters. Type a task, a person or a project, or pick a chip such as Due today.

The basics

Write the task, choose who it is for, then Add task. Due, Project and Priority are underneath. While you type, similar tasks that already exist are listed so you don’t add a duplicate.

MBC and Individual

An MBC task is a team task, shown to everyone and counted in Report. An Individual task is personal and stays out of the team numbers.

Each person’s part

After you choose people, you can write what each person is doing. It shows on the task.

Project

Pick an existing project or type a new name. A project appears in Projects as soon as a task uses it.

All tasks

At the bottom of the page. Open it to see every task, filter by date range, and edit or delete. Deleting asks for the admin password.

Plan a communication

Tap a day. Add a subject, pick a type and list the people involved. Switch on Also add it as a task to create a matching team task due that day.

Types

Each type has its own shape: Video triangle, Blog square, Email diamond, Event disc, Social plus, Livestream ring, Other dash. The chips under the month show one type at a time.

Status

Tap an item’s status to cycle Planned, Sent, Delayed. Delayed shows as late.

The year and the view

Communications for the year are listed at the bottom, month by month. Landscape view shows subjects in each day on a wide screen. Everyone sees the same calendar, live.

Switching projects

The chips at the top switch project. A dot on a chip means it has overdue tasks.

What you see

A progress ring, then open tasks with in progress first and the soonest due next. On hold, Done and Team are folded underneath.

Actions

Add task opens Add with the project filled in. Edit changes the description and target date. Mark complete moves the project under Completed projects, with an Undo. Reopen brings it back.

New project

Use New project to start one before it has any tasks. A project’s name can’t change afterwards, because every task uses it.

Four views

Quarterly is a quarter at a glance. Monthly lists a month’s team tasks, with filters. Weekly is this week’s digest. Performance shows one person or one project over a week, month, quarter or overall.

Share

The Share button copies a summary or opens a printable report you can save as a PDF. Which options appear depends on the view.

What counts

Only MBC tasks count in the team numbers. Individual tasks stay out.

Quick notes

A private scratchpad in your profile on Home. It syncs between your devices.

Reminders

Open a task and tap Remind me. When it is due you get a sound and a notice, and you can snooze or dismiss it.

Focus timer

Start it from your profile on Home. While it runs, the time left shows at the top.

Install as an app

On Android, open the site in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Refresh

On Home, pull down from the top to refresh. Updates also arrive on their own.
